# Evaluation figures

The screenshots in this directory were recorded during the E3--E5 GUI runs.
Their original files are kept in `/home/shikai/Pictures`; the copies here use
stable names referenced by `chapters/evaluation.tex`.

- `e3_a.png`, `e3_b.png`, `e3_b_fails.png`, `e3_c_start.png`, and
  `e3_c_fails.png` show the three stages of the E3 mission.
- `e4_meet_rviz.png`, `e4_meet_first.png`, and `e4_meet_second.png` show the
  two E4 crossings in Gazebo and the two namespaced RViz views.
- `e5_gui_ready.png`, `e5_start.jpg`, `e5_first_meet.jpg`,
  `e5_first_clean.jpg`, `e5_second_meet.jpg`, and `e5_second_clean.jpg` show
  the manually started E5 illustration run.
- `e5_pi_wired.jpg` shows the Raspberry Pi connected by Ethernet to the LAN
  router used for E5.
